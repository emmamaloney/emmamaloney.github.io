About

Education

Currently I'm starting my 3rd year of studies of Engineering Mathematics at the Royal Institute of Technology (KTH) in Stockholm, Sweden.

Prior to university studies, I graduated from the natural sciences program in sixth form with 22.5/22.5 (all A's).

I have also completed some extra programming courses during summers.

Work Experience

During the spring term of 2026, I was a teaching assistant for the course Fundamentals of Computer Science for Scientific Computing (DD1328), at the university I study at (KTH). This allowed me to hone my skills in the area, as I helped students during assignments and subsequently evaluated oral examinations.

Alongside my core professional pursuit, I have worked a variety of jobs during summers and my sabbatical year, including store associate (café + retail) at Biltema, hostel staff in Gran Canaria, cleaner at Hotel Tylösand, all-rounder at Strandgården, and remote freelance work for translation and copy writing via Fiverr. This stems from my constant drive to tackle new problems and my comfort with being highly adaptive.

Professional Philosophy

The skills within mathematics, data science and machine learning can be simplified to the idea of tools in a tool box. To learn a new skill is to expand your collection, and with every addition you are capable of solving more problems. This, in combination with the principle "Learn – Learn more", inspires the premise that work in this field encourages learning.
